# Feetech HD-1910-C001 (a.k.a. HD1910M): servo spec

Compiled 2026-09-30. Feetech has published no official datasheet for this part. The values below come from
retailer listings and from a community build that tested the servo on real hardware. Every value is tagged with
its source:

- **[R1]** lxrcmodel.com HD1910M listing (the fullest spec table):
  https://lxrcmodel.com/product/feetech-hd1910m-6v-12kg-metal-gear-coreless-ttl-serial-bus-servo-diy-microduck-open-source-robot-actuator/
- **[R2]** OpenELAB HD-1910-C001 listing: https://openelab.io/products/feetech-hd1910-microduck-coreless-servo
- **[C]** jyg9/microduck-community-kit, `docs/飞特通讯协议说明.md`: the Feetech HLS-series memory table, plus
  tests on real HD-1910-C001 units (fw 3.46, 1 Mbps, 2026-09-24): https://github.com/jyg9/microduck-community-kit
- **[D]** derived here from the values above. These are estimates, not measurements.

**Derived simple DC-motor model, output side, 6 V [D]** (for sim actuator parameters; replace with a fit):

- Torque constant incl. gearing, Kt ≈ 1.18 N·m / 1.6 A ≈ **0.74 N·m/A**
- Terminal resistance incl. driver, R ≈ 6 V / 1.6 A ≈ **3.75 Ω**
- Back-EMF, ke ≈ (6 − 0.18·3.75) / 9.6 ≈ **0.55 V·s/rad**
- Linear torque-speed line: τ_max(ω) ≈ 1.18 · (1 − |ω| / 9.6) N·m at 6 V. Both ends scale roughly with supply
  voltage.

No BAM (Rhoban better-actuator-models) fit exists for this servo yet. Rhoban/bam ships fits for xl330, xl320,
mx64, mx106, feetech_sts3215_7_4V, waveshare_st3025 and erob only.

## Bus / protocol [C]

- **Protocol: Feetech SCS/HLS** (not STS). Half-duplex TTL, 8N1, header `FF FF ID LEN INST …`, checksum
  `~(ID+LEN+INST+params) & 0xFF`, little-endian.
- Model number read back from a real unit: `0x1F0A`, firmware 3.46.
- Baud rate (reg 6): 0=1M, 1=500k, 2=250k, 3=128k, 4=115200, 5=76800, 6=57600, 7=38400.
- Instructions: 0x01 PING, 0x02 READ, 0x03 WRITE, 0x04 REG_WRITE, 0x05 ACTION, 0x08 REBOOT (no reply; back in
  ~823 ms), 0x0A RESET, 0x0B CAL (sets the current position as center), 0x82 SYNC_READ, 0x83 SYNC_WRITE (no reply).
- In a sync_read, each ID listed before a servo delays that servo's reply by ~295 µs.

## Open questions

- The stall torque is either 10 or 12 kg·cm, depending on the source. OpenELAB has no torque variants (its only
  option is servo vs URT-2 test board) and gives no test voltage. 12 kg·cm at 6 V scaled to 5 V is exactly
  10 kg·cm, so the gap may just be test voltage. [R1] names the part "HD1910M", not "-C001". On the STS3215,
  the -Cxxx suffix marks gear-ratio/voltage variants, so the two listings may not describe identical units.
  Until a bench stall test settles it, use 0.98 N·m in sim.
- The max-speed and protection-current defaults for this model are unconfirmed.
- Backlash, friction, rotor inertia and thermal limits have not been measured.
